# Skill: Family Law Hooks  — read `../hook-library.md` first

`motion_baseline: conservative` — governs the visual tier in `remotion-viral-reels` (matrix lookup).

## Energy / tone
Emotional stability, empathy, protection, calm authority. This niche must NEVER sound like PI.
No aggression, no "destroy your ex." Emotional intelligence first — kids are usually involved.

## Trigger weighting (override)
Protection 25 · Validation 25 · Hope 20 · Authority 15 · Curiosity 15 · Fear 0.
LinkedIn variant: Authority → 35, calm.

## What works — patterns (fill from brand.md + Local Intelligence Profile)
- Validation: "You're not a bad parent for wanting to protect your kids in this."
- Protection: "The financial mistake that quietly hurts parents in a divorce."
- Calm authority: "Here's what actually happens in the first custody hearing."
- Hope: "It feels like you'll never untangle this. Here's the first step that helps."
- Curiosity: "The one text message that changes a custody case."

## Local / geographic
SOFT intel only — county custody tendencies, local court delays — angle only. Keep it humane.

## Escalation (gentle, trust-safe)
L1 "This can complicate your case." → L2 "This mistake can affect custody." →
L3 "It's a mistake the other side's lawyer will use." (stop — no fear-farming)

## Banned in family (on top of global)
No villainizing an ex/spouse (kids + optics); no guaranteeing custody/support outcomes; no exploiting
grief or fear; no aggressive/vindictive framing; no specific case hijacking. Empathy over outrage.

## Platform notes
TikTok/IG: validation + calm guidance. Shorts: "how custody works in [state]". Facebook: warm,
community, protective. LinkedIn: measured authority.

## Bilingual
If `es` active, parallel ES (`-es`) — family content is highly shareable in ES communities.
