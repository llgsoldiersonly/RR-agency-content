# Skill: Personal Injury Hooks  — read `../hook-library.md` first

## Energy / tone
Protective, justice-oriented, confident, urgent. Anti-insurance-*tactics* — never anti-people,
never absolute villains, never ambulance-chasing or fear-farming. Plain-spoken.

## Trigger weighting (PI override of the default)
Curiosity 25 · Authority 20 · Protection 20 · Fear 20 (urgency/stakes, trust-safe) · Validation 10 · Hope 5.
PI tolerates more urgency than family/probate — kept trust-safe.
LinkedIn variant: Fear → 0, Authority → 40 (insight for referring attorneys).

## What works in PI — patterns (fill brackets from brand.md + Local Intelligence Profile)
- Self-sabotage (Curiosity+Fear): "Most people hurt their own claim before they ever call a lawyer. Here's the first mistake."
- Insurer-tactic reveal (Authority): "Here's what that first call from their insurer is really for."
- Local pattern (Protection+local): "[local highway/intersection] sees more serious crashes than people think — here's what to do if it's you."
- Don't-do-this (Curiosity+urgency): "After a crash, do NOT do this — even if you feel totally fine."
- Process authority (Authority): "Here's how we actually build a [crash type] case." (no dollar promises)
- Validation (Validation): "If an adjuster made you feel like the crash was your fault — watch this."
- Hope/resolution (Hope): "Even if you think it's too late to do anything about your injury — it might not be."

## Geographic (use the Local Intelligence Profile)
Named highways/intersections, local weather-driving patterns (e.g., FL summer storms + hydroplaning),
seasonal traffic (tourist/snowbird season). State as fact ONLY if it's in the profile.

## Escalation (PI, trust-safe)
L1 "This can hurt your claim." → L2 "This mistake can cut what you recover." →
L3 "It's the mistake the insurer is hoping you make." (stop)

## Banned in PI (on top of global)
No specific crash/news hijacking; no settlement/verdict figures unless in results.md; no "insurance
is evil" absolutes; don't traumatize people who are already injured/anxious — activate, don't scare.

## Platform notes
TikTok/IG: mistake + curiosity hooks. Shorts: lead with "what to do after a car accident in [city]".
Facebook: protective/local. LinkedIn: process authority + injury-law insight for referral attorneys.

## Bilingual
If `es` is active, write parallel ES hooks (own id, `-es` suffix) — localized, not translated.
ES audiences over-index on Facebook + WhatsApp.
