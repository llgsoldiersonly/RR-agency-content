# Skill: Criminal Defense Hooks  — read `../hook-library.md` first

## Energy / tone
Immediate, calm, high-stakes, reassuring. Authority that lowers the viewer's panic, not raises it.
The audience is often scared — be the steady voice. Never imply you can beat any charge.

## Trigger weighting (override)
Authority 25 · Hope 20 · Curiosity 20 · Protection 15 (family of the accused) · Fear 15 (rights stakes, CALM) · Validation 5.
LinkedIn variant: Authority → 40, Fear → 0.

## What works — patterns (fill from brand.md + Local Intelligence Profile)
- Rights protection (Authority+Fear, calm): "If you're ever pulled over, here's the one sentence that protects you."
- After-arrest mistakes (Curiosity): "The first thing people do after an arrest that quietly wrecks their case."
- Never-say-this (Authority): "Never say this to an officer — even if you're innocent."
- Hope/path (Hope): "A charge is not a conviction. Here's what actually happens next."
- Family protection (Protection): "If someone you love was just arrested, do these 3 things tonight."

## Local / geographic
Mostly SOFT intel — county prosecution tendencies, local court timing — and it shapes the ANGLE only.
HARD facts (where a courthouse is) are fine. NEVER state checkpoint locations or imply you can predict
or game a specific judge/prosecutor.

## Escalation (calm, trust-safe)
L1 "This can hurt your defense." → L2 "This mistake can cost you options later." →
L3 "It's the mistake prosecutors count on." (stop — never "we'll get it dismissed")

## Banned in criminal (on top of global)
No implying guaranteed dismissal/acquittal; no implying you can manipulate a named judge/prosecutor;
no coaching anyone to evade lawful enforcement or obstruct; no specific arrest/crime hijacking;
reassure, never promise. Don't amplify fear in an already-scared audience.

## Platform notes
TikTok/IG: rights + "never say this." Shorts: "what to do after a DUI arrest in [city]". Facebook:
calm/family. LinkedIn: defense-strategy insight, professional.

## Bilingual
If `es` active, write parallel ES hooks (`-es`). Rights content travels well across language.
