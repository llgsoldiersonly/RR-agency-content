# Skill: Workers' Comp Hooks  — read `../hook-library.md` first

`motion_baseline: medium` — governs the visual tier in `remotion-viral-reels` (matrix lookup).

## Energy / tone
Worker advocacy, protection, righteous (trust-safe) frustration with employer/insurer TACTICS — not
"all employers are evil." Validate the injured worker; empower them with their rights.

## Trigger weighting (override)
Validation 25 · Protection 20 · Curiosity 20 · Fear 15 (employer manipulation / retaliation, trust-safe) · Authority 15 · Hope 5.
LinkedIn variant: Authority → 35.

## What works — patterns (fill from brand.md)
- Hidden rights (Authority+Curiosity): "Things your employer can't legally do after you're hurt at work."
- Validation: "If they made you feel like your injury isn't 'real' — you're not faking, and here's proof."
- Tactic reveal (trust-safe): "What the claims adjuster is really doing when they're 'just checking in.'"
- Denied-claim hope (Hope): "A denied claim is not the end. Here's the next move."
- Don't-do-this (Curiosity): "The mistake that quietly sinks work-injury claims."

## Local / geographic — LOW
Workers' comp is STATE-statute-driven. Lead state-level; benefits vary by state, so don't state a
right as universal when it's state-specific.

## Escalation (trust-safe)
L1 "This can hurt your claim." → L2 "This mistake can get a valid claim denied." →
L3 "It's exactly what the insurer is hoping you do." (stop)

## Banned in workers comp (on top of global)
No guaranteeing claim approval/benefit amounts; no "all employers screw workers" absolutes; no naming
a specific employer (defamation); no encouraging fraud; flag state-law-varies rather than stating a
benefit as universal.

## Platform notes
TikTok/IG: "your rights" + validation. Shorts: "[state] workers comp explained". Facebook: worker
advocacy/community. LinkedIn: workplace-injury insight.

## Bilingual
If `es` active, parallel ES (`-es`) — strong fit for ES-speaking labor workforce; favor FB/WhatsApp.
