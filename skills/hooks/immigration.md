# Skill: Immigration Hooks  — read `../hook-library.md` first

`motion_baseline: conservative` — governs the visual tier in `remotion-viral-reels` (matrix lookup).

## Energy / tone
Hope, clarity, reassurance, gentle urgency. The audience is ALREADY anxious — give certainty, never
amplify fear. Avoid robotic informational tone. Warm, human, authoritative-but-kind.

## Trigger weighting (override)
Hope 30 · Protection 20 · Authority 20 · Curiosity 15 · Validation 15 · Fear 0.
LinkedIn variant: Authority → 40 (employer/business immigration angle).

## What works — patterns (fill from brand.md + Local Intelligence Profile)
- Hidden pathway (Hope+Curiosity): "You might qualify for a path you've never heard of."
- Clarity (Authority): "Here's what actually happens after you file — step by step."
- Reassurance (Hope): "Feeling stuck in the process doesn't mean it's over. Here's why."
- Mistake-avoidance (Authority): "The small application mistake that delays cases for months."
- Family (Protection): "Keeping your family together — where most people get the timing wrong."

## Local / geographic
SOFT/community — local immigrant communities, common regional visa/employment pathways — for relevance
and trust. Keep it supportive.

## Escalation (gentle, trust-safe)
L1 "This can slow your case down." → L2 "This mistake delays a lot of applications." →
L3 "It's a common, fixable mistake — here's how to avoid it." (resolve, don't scare)

## Banned in immigration (on top of global)
NEVER promise or imply approval / a green card / citizenship outcomes. No deportation/raid fear-bait
to scare people into calling (predatory — this audience is vulnerable). No specific enforcement-event
hijacking. Hope and certainty, never fear-exploitation.

## Bilingual — ESSENTIAL
Default to producing BOTH EN and localized ES (and other relevant languages where the firm serves
them). ES audiences over-index on Facebook + WhatsApp; prioritize those for ES variants.

## Platform notes
TikTok/IG: hope + "you might qualify". Shorts: "[visa/process] explained". Facebook/WhatsApp:
community trust (esp. ES). LinkedIn: business/employment immigration.
