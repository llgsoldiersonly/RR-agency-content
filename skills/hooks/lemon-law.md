# Skill: Lemon Law Hooks  — read `../hook-library.md` first

`motion_baseline: medium` — governs the visual tier in `remotion-viral-reels` (matrix lookup).

## Energy / tone
Consumer empowerment + righteous (trust-safe) outrage + validation. "You may be owed something and
not know it." Energizing, not unhinged. Anti-defective-product-tactics, not "all dealers are crooks."

## Trigger weighting (override)
Validation 25 · Curiosity 20 · Authority 20 · Fear 20 (here = righteous outrage / "you're being shorted," trust-safe) · Protection 10 · Hope 5.
LinkedIn variant: Authority → 35.

## What works — patterns (fill from brand.md)
- Hidden entitlement (Curiosity+Validation): "You might be owed a refund or buyback and have no idea."
- Tactic reveal (Authority, trust-safe): "Here's what the manufacturer is hoping you don't realize."
- Validation: "If your 'new' car has been in the shop 3+ times — you're not overreacting."
- What-counts (Authority): "What actually makes a car a lemon (it's more than you think)."
- Empowerment (Hope): "You don't have to just live with a defective car. You have options."

## Local / geographic — LOW
Lemon law is STATE-statute-driven, not city-driven. Lead state-level ("[State]'s lemon law is
stronger than people think"), not local intersections. Don't force geography that isn't there.

## Escalation (trust-safe)
L1 "This could mean you're owed money." → L2 "Most owners never claim what they're entitled to." →
L3 "It's exactly what the manufacturer is counting on." (stop)

## Banned in lemon law (on top of global)
No guaranteeing a refund/buyback amount; no "all dealers/all of [brand] are crooks" (defamation risk);
don't tell people to stop car payments as flat legal advice (frame as "you may have options"); no
naming a brand as always selling lemons.

## Platform notes
TikTok/IG: "you might be owed money" curiosity. Shorts: "[state] lemon law explained". Facebook:
consumer-advocacy. LinkedIn: consumer-protection insight.
