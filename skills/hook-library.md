# Skill: Hook Engine (shared) — READ THIS before any practice-area hook file

**Objective:** engineer locally-anchored, *emotionally-activated authority* — content that makes
the firm the recognized local legal authority (branded search, referrals, map engagement), NOT
viral influencer content. Optimize for trust + recognition + signed cases, not raw retention.

**Router:** based on the item's practice area (from `brand.md`), open `skills/hooks/<area>.md`
(personal-injury, criminal, family, lemon-law, immigration, probate, workers-comp). That file
carries the area's energy, weighting deltas, patterns, and local-intel use. THIS file carries
the rules that apply to every area.

## The model: controlled tension ("trust-safe tension")
Not sterile authority (dies on short-form) and not fear/rage-bait (wins views, loses trust,
attracts tire-kickers). The zone is emotionally-activated authority.

A hook MAY activate: a common mistake, a hidden cost, real stakes, curiosity, local relevance.
A hook may NOT:
- name an absolute villain ("Insurance companies LOVE screwing you")
- promise or imply an outcome ("we'll win you X")
- reference a specific real recent event, accident, crime, or victim (no tragedy hijacking)
- shame the viewer

### The rewrite transform (run it on any too-hot hook)
Shift *villain-blame → common, fixable mistake the viewer controls.*
- BAD: "Insurance companies love screwing accident victims."
- GOOD: "Most people quietly hurt their own claim on the first call with insurance — here's the one line to avoid."
Same activation, agency on the viewer, no villain, fixable.

## Trigger weighting = a MATRIX, not one list
House default: Curiosity 25 · Authority 25 · Protection 20 · Validation 15 · Hope 10 · Fear 5.
Each practice-area file OVERRIDES this row. Then apply the platform overlay below. Never ship one
global mix — that re-flattens the differentiation.

## Platform tone modulation (one idea ≠ one tone)
- TikTok — discovery: curiosity/tension highest, fastest hook, most creator-native.
- IG Reels — familiarity: same energy, slightly more branded.
- YT Shorts — searchable authority: lead with the keyword/question.
- Facebook — local trust: warmer, community, protection-led.
- LinkedIn — referral network: Authority up, Fear → 0, no "comment WORD 👇", professional.
- Google Business Profile — entity reinforcement: plain, local, authoritative.

## Geographic anchoring (pull from the Local Intelligence Profile in brand.md)
Generic ("If you drive in [city]…") is the weak version. Strong hooks use real local specifics.
- Use ONLY what the firm supplied — never invent local facts (same rule as results.md).
- HARD local color (real roads, intersections, weather/seasonal patterns, community hubs) → state as fact.
- SOFT intel (court/prosecutor/judge tendencies) → shapes the ANGLE only; never stated as insider
  fact, never implying you can game a named judge/prosecutor.

## Escalation ladder (capped at trust-safe)
L1 "This can quietly hurt your case." → L2 "This one mistake can shrink what you recover." →
L3 "It's the mistake the other side is counting on." Stop at L3. Never cross into absolute-villain
or outcome-promise.

## Banned globally
Dead educational framing ("Understanding the probate process", "Today we discuss…", "What is
workers comp?"); tragedy/event hijacking; absolute-villain framing; outcome promises/guarantees;
viewer-shaming; naming competitors.

## Classification tags (attach to every content.json item)
Add a `tags` object: `{ trigger, hookType, funnelStage, localRelevance, platformFit, perfGoal }`.
V1 = tag for organization. V2 = correlate tags against real performance to learn what wins by
area/city (the long-term moat). Do NOT build analytics machinery in V1 — just tag.
