# Skill: Carousel Generator

Output 5–7 slides into the item's `slides` array (each `heading` + `body`), plus `caption`,
`hashtags`, `cta`.

## Shape
- Slide 1 = the hook (cover). Big promise or sharp question.
- Slides 2–6 = one point each, scannable. `heading` is the takeaway; `body` is 1–2 lines.
- Last slide = CTA + save/share prompt.

## Rules
- Carousels are for saves — make each slide quotable on its own.
- Numbers/results only from results.md.
- Keep body lines short enough to render on a 1080×1350 card.
